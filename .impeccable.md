# Design Context

## Target Audience
- Administrators who coordinate admissions, fees, rooms, staffing, and operations
- Caregiving staff who need fast access to elder records, health information, and daily tasks

## Use Cases
- Manage elder profiles, admissions, departures, and bed occupancy
- Track health records, care services, accidents, and visits
- Handle reservations, fees, inventory, and other daily operating workflows

## Brand Personality And Tone
- Professional and trustworthy
- Warm and reassuring rather than cold or corporate
- Clear, efficient, and healthcare-service oriented

## Interface Direction
- Light theme for daytime operational use in care facilities
- Soft medical-service palette with sage, linen, and warm neutral surfaces
- Calm hierarchy that reduces visual noise while keeping dense data readable
